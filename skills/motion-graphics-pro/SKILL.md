# Motion Graphics & Graphic Design Pro (10/10 Production Standard)

This skill dictates the visual, motion, and graphic production standards to ensure all creative assets achieve a **10/10 aesthetic score**.

---

## 1. Graphic Design: The 10/10 Visual Foundation

### Color Theory & Luminescence
- **The 60-30-10 Rule:**
  - **60% Base / Dominant:** Deep Obsidian Matte (`#080B11` / `#05070C`). Never washed-out gray.
  - **30% Structural Surface:** Layered glassmorphic cards (`#0F172A` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` borders).
  - **10% Kinetic Accent:** Glowing Electric Cyan (`#38BDF8`) or Emerald (`#34D399`). Accents must draw the focal eye within 200ms.
- **Contrast Ratios:** Minimum 7:1 for headers, 4.5:1 for body copy. Never low-contrast gray text on dark gray.

### Typography Mastery
- **Pairing Rule:** One high-impact grotesque/geometric sans for display (`Inter Display`, `Outfit`, or `Syne`) paired with a high-legibility monospace for technical metadata (`JetBrains Mono`, `Fira Code`).
- **Tracking & Leading:**
  - Display Headlines (>48px): Tight tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.03em`), line height `1.05 - 1.15`.
  - Body Copy (24px - 36px): Neutral tracking (`0em`), line height `1.45 - 1.55`.
  - Labels & Badges (<20px): Wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`), all-caps.
- **Maximum 3 Sizes:** Never exceed 3 distinct type scales on a single slide or video overlay.

### Composition & Safe Zones
- **Portrait Canvas:** 1080x1350 for Carousels (4:5), 1080x1920 for Reels (9:16).
- **The 120px Buffer:** Keep critical titles, text, and logos at least 120px away from all edges to avoid Instagram UI clipping (profile header, caption gradient, like/share sidebar).

## 2. Motion Design & Animation Physics

### Physics & Easing
- **Ban Linear Motion:** Every element must accelerate and decelerate with physical intent.
- **The Golden Spring Curve:** `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (out-expo). Snappy entry, butter-smooth deceleration.
- **Micro-Overshoot:** Spring physics with damping ratio `0.75 - 0.82` for interactive badges and UI cards.

### The 2.5-Second Visual Reset Rule (Reels)
- **Retention Driver:** Human eyes disengage after 2.5 to 3 seconds of static frames.
- **Execution:** Every 2.5 seconds, trigger at least one visual change:
  1. Camera punch-in (1.0x -> 1.08x scale).
  2. Terminal command execution with glowing syntax.
  3. Overlay card pop-in.
  4. B-roll cut or split-screen transition.

### Kinetic Typography
- Words appear in lockstep with the spoken voiceover (zero desync).
- Active spoken word is highlighted in cyan/emerald or scales 1.05x.
- Keep on-screen captions to 1 to 3 words per flash for maximum dwell velocity.

## 3. Audio & Sound Design Layering

A 10/10 visual fails if sound design is flat:
1. **Voiceover Track:** Mastered to `-1.5 dB` to `-3.0 dB` with light compression, noise gate, and low-end warmth (100–150Hz boost).
2. **Audio Ducking:** Background synth/lo-fi music ducks automatically by `-14 dB` whenever the voiceover is active.
3. **Foley & UI SFX:**
   - Subtle digital "tick" on code execution.
   - Low-frequency "whoosh" (sub-bass sweep) on major slide transitions.
   - SFX must sit at `-12 dB` relative to dialogue (felt, not overpowering).

---

## 4. The 10/10 Pre-Flight Audit Checklist

Before releasing any creative asset, verify all 6 checks:
- [ ] **Grid Check:** Is the title legible at 200x200px thumbnail size?
- [ ] **Safe Zone Check:** Is all copy clear of Instagram's bottom caption and right action bar?
- [ ] **Contrast Check:** Does every slide pass 7:1 contrast on display text?
- [ ] **Pacing Check:** Is there a visual change or camera motion every 2.5 seconds?
- [ ] **Sound Check:** Is the background track ducked cleanly under speech?
- [ ] **Loop Check:** Does the final frame/line transition smoothly back into the opener?
