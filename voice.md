## Core Positions

1. Free AI models and open web tools can accomplish 90% of tasks without expensive monthly subscriptions.

3. Sustainable digital income comes from solving real micro-problems and sharing practical resources, not get-rich-quick hype.

## Off Limits

- **Topics to avoid:** Crypto pump-and-dump schemes, get-rich-quick gambling, unverified download links.

## Proof & Credibility

- Free web tools demonstrated directly in the browser.
- Real before-and-after workflow time savings.
- Clear step-by-step action items viewers can test immediately for $0.
