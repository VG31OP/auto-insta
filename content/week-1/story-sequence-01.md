# Daily Story Sequence 01: The Automation Audit

- **Surface:** Instagram Stories (1080x1920 9:16)
- **Goal:** Drive direct message conversations, identify warm leads, and increase follower engagement.
- **Timing:** Post between 8:30 AM - 10:30 AM local time.

---

## 4-Frame Story Blueprint

### Frame 1: The Open
- **Visual:** Real photo or clip of your desk/laptop setup with dark mode terminal running green/cyan code in the background. Hand visible typing a keystroke.
- **Text (Centered, top half):**  
  `Building the 6 AM automation pipeline.`
- **Safe Zone:** y=350 to y=600.

### Frame 2: The Insight
- **Visual:** Quick 3-second screencast showing terminal output: `Finished in 11.8s. 0 manual clicks.`
- **Text:**  
  `The biggest myth in tech right now is that you need a 5-person team to handle research and data entry.`

### Frame 3: The Poll (The Engagement Driver)
- **Visual:** Still screenshot of clean workflow architecture diagram.
- **Interactive Sticker:** **POLL STICKER**
  - **Question:** *How many hours do you spend on repetitive copy-paste tasks every week?*
  - **Option A:** *Under 2 hours*
  - **Option B:** *5 to 10+ hours*

### Frame 4: The Lead DM Funnel
- **Visual:** Dark background with terminal prompt.
- **Text:**  
  `If you answered 5+ hours: DM me "FLOW" and I'll send over the 20-line script I use to run this completely on autopilot.`
- **Interactive Sticker:** **DM ME STICKER** or Keyword Prompt: `FLOW`

---

## Follow-up Action
Anyone who votes *5 to 10+ hours* on Frame 3 or views Frame 4 should receive the `FLOW` keyword message within 2 hours.
