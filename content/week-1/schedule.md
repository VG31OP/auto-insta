## Daily 15-Minute Engagement Routine (Before Posting)

Engage with 5-10 accounts in the `#aitools`, `#techhacks`, and `#productivity` niches:
- Target reels and posts under 2 hours old to catch the algorithmic surge.
- Leave insightful, high-value comments using `/ig-comment` (observations, added tips, or contrarian points).
- Never post generic emoji spam. Real discourse boosts your account trust score.
